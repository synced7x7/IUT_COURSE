Hadith Qudsi, also known as Sacred Hadith or Divine Hadith, are a category of sayings and actions of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) in Islamic tradition. However, they differ from regular Hadith in that they convey the words of Allah as revealed to the Prophet, but they are not part of the Quran. Here are some key points about Hadith Qudsi

1. Divine Revelation Hadith Qudsi are considered a form of divine revelation, where Allah's message is conveyed through the words of the Prophet Muhammad. They provide insights into Allah's thoughts, feelings, and intentions.

2. Distinctive Characteristic Unlike the Quran, which is considered the literal word of Allah, Hadith Qudsi are the Prophet's narration of Allah's words and are considered indirect divine revelations.

3. Authenticity Just like regular Hadith, the authenticity of Hadith Qudsi is critical. Islamic scholars use rigorous criteria to authenticate and verify these sayings to ensure their reliability.

4. Topics Hadith Qudsi can cover various topics, including morality, ethics, guidance, and the relationship between humans and Allah. They often emphasize the importance of faith, repentance, gratitude, and patience.

5. Spiritual Guidance These Hadith serve as a source of spiritual guidance for Muslims, helping them understand Allah's expectations and how to lead a life in accordance with Islamic principles.

6. Recitation Muslims may recite Hadith Qudsi as part of their religious rituals, supplications, and prayers to seek spiritual closeness to Allah.

7. Not a Replacement for the Quran Hadith Qudsi are not a substitute for the Quran. While they provide valuable insights, they do not carry the same level of authority and significance as the Quranic verses.

8. Collection Hadith Qudsi are found in various Hadith collections, with Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim being among the most reputable sources.

9. Example An example of a Hadith Qudsi is the saying where Allah says, O son of Adam, so long as you call upon Me and ask of Me, I shall forgive you for what you have done, and I shall not mind. This Hadith emphasizes Allah's mercy and the importance of supplication.

In summary, Hadith Qudsi are a unique category of Hadith in Islam, representing Allah's words as conveyed through the Prophet Muhammad. They offer spiritual guidance and insights into the divine will while remaining distinct from the Quran in terms of their status and authority.

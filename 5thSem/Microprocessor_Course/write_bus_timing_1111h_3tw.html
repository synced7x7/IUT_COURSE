8086 WRITE Bus Timing (Complete BUS Cycle)

I/O Write · Port Address = 1111h · 3 Wait States · @ 5 MHz (200 ns/clock)
Port Address: 1111h
Operation: I/O WRITE → M/IO̅ = LOW
Wait States: 3 × TW
Total T-States: T1+T2+TW×3+T3+T4 = 7
Total Time: 7 × 200ns = 1400ns
A₁₉-A₁₆: 0000 (I/O — upper addr unused)

Bus Timing for I/O Write Operation — 8086 @ 5 MHz — Port 1111h — 3 Wait States

① Port 1111h → I/O Write M/IO̅ = LOW throughout the entire bus cycle (I/O operation, not memory). Port address 1111h appears on AD₁₅-AD₀ during T1. A₁₉-A₁₆ = 0000 since I/O only uses 16-bit addressing.
② 3 Wait States (TW×3) READY sampled LOW at end of T2 → TW₁ inserted. READY still LOW → TW₂, TW₃ inserted. READY goes HIGH → T3 executes normally. Total = 7 clocks = 1400 ns @ 5MHz.
③ ALE — Address Latch Enable Pulses HIGH during T1 only. External latch (e.g. 8282) captures port address 1111h before AD₁₅-AD₀ switches to carry data in TW/T3.
④ DT/R̅ = HIGH (Transmit) CPU is sending data OUT to port. DT/R̅ stays HIGH from T1 through T4. Directs 8286/8287 bus transceiver to pass data from CPU toward the I/O port.
⑤ WR̅ — Write Control Goes LOW at T2, stays LOW through all 3 TW and T3. The trailing edge (LOW→HIGH) at end of T4 is the critical moment — data is officially committed and written to port 1111h.
⑥ DEN̅ — Data Enable Goes LOW at T2 to enable the data bus transceiver. Stays LOW through all wait states and T3. Returns HIGH at T4 end — bus released for next cycle.
